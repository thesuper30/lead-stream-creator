# Meet Our Super 30 Homepage Section

## Goal
Replace the current “The Super 30 Difference” comparison block with a highly visual, interactive team catalogue using the existing verified team photos, names, and specialties.

## Build
- Introduce the section with “Meet Our Super 30,” a concise team-led message, and a link to the full About page.
- Show team members in an editorial image grid with varied card sizes, layered name/specialty details, and a strong visual rhythm.
- Add category filters so visitors can quickly explore strategy, search, performance, technology, and creative specialists.
- Add polished interaction: image movement, focus states, active filters, and a selected-member spotlight without disruptive popups.
- Keep the site’s current light palette, orange brand accents, typography, rounded CTA style, and mobile behavior.

## Technical Details
- Reuse the 14 real team portraits and the confirmed names/specialties already used on the About page.
- Keep the change isolated to the homepage presentation and preserve all surrounding sections, links, content, and tracking.
- Ensure keyboard accessibility, reduced-motion support, stable image dimensions, and clean two-column/mobile stacking.
- Verify the final section in desktop and mobile previews for clipping, overlap, interaction, and horizontal overflow.
